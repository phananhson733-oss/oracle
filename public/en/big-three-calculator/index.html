Core trio at a glance

Your "Big Three" are your Sun, Moon, and rising (ascendant) signs — the three placements astrologers reach for first. Together they sketch your identity, your emotional life, and the style others first meet you with.

Sun, Moon, and rising roles

The Sun is your core identity and will; the Moon is your emotional instincts and needs; the rising is your outward manner and first impressions. Most people are a blend — the three rarely sit in the same sign, which is why a one-line horoscope never quite fits.
