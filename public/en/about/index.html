About AstrologyWiki

AstrologyWiki is a modern, psychology-grounded astrology knowledge base built on real astronomy — no mysticism, no fortune-telling.

AstrologyWiki pairs Swiss Ephemeris astronomy with modern psychology to make astrology a tool for self-knowledge rather than prediction. Our wiki, calculators, and CBT journal are free to use. Learn more about our editorial approach, data sources, and the people behind the project.
