Why It Matters for Self-Awareness

Most searches for this keeper's chart arrive hoping a Gemini Sun will explain a save or call the next result. That is the wrong question, and it is where readers get stuck, because a Sun sign was never built to predict a match. The lens that helps is the one Steven Forrest is known for: read a placement as a set of growth themes a person can grow into, not a script that decides their day. Held that way, the reading is not a forecast of clean sheets; it frames a study in adaptability — how a veteran reads a moving game and steadies the players in front of him.

That framing matters most when the story is an underdog one. Cape Verde, an archipelago of half a million people, reached its first-ever World Cup — the third-smallest nation ever to qualify, behind only Curaçao and Iceland — having topped a qualifying group above Cameroon and Angola.
