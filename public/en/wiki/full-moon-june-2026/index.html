Why is June 29 considered the wrong day to launch a new project?

Any project or intention initiated on June 29 enters Mercury retrograde's shadow immediately, meaning it is likely to need revisiting or renegotiating within the following 25 days. What ritual actually fits this full moon?

A ritual built around honest audit works with this configuration: review what you've built or planned over the past six months, identify what has genuinely produced results and what hasn't, and write a deliberate release focused on the structures and plans that no longer serve your actual direction.

What does Mercury stationing retrograde on this full moon mean in practice?
