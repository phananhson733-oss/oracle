Common Questions About the Kevin De Bruyne Natal Chart

What is Kevin De Bruyne's Sun sign?

His Sun is in Cancer, based on a birth date of June 28, 1991 in Drongen, Belgium. This is the one placement nearly every listing agrees on, because it depends on the date alone and not on a birth time.

Why are his Moon and Rising signs marked "approximate"?

The commonly cited Capricorn Moon and Virgo Rising rely on unconfirmed birth-time sources rather than a documented record. Without a verified time of birth, those two layers stay informed estimates that can shift, so careful reads flag them rather than stating them flatly.

Does the birth chart explain his skill on the pitch?

No — that is the line worth keeping clear. The chart offers a symbolic framework for how his on-pitch persona tends to read to fans, not a cause of his passing, assists, or trophies.

How is a birth chart different from a kundli?

They describe the same natal map through two traditions: "kundli" is the term used in Vedic astrology, while "birth chart" is the common Western label. The underlying placements are the same, but they are read through different house and sign systems, which is why the two can emphasize different details.
